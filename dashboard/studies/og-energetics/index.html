Proto-Energetics: Can a System Capture and Couple Free Energy?

Q Can a primitive chemical system capture geochemical free energy, internalize it under selection, and couple a specified carrier to perform endergonic work — and does any of it improve total throughput, or only persistence/robustness — each tested against a matched null with a closing ledger?

Claim

Across fidelities, a protocell's energetics buy robustness/persistence, not raw throughput. A primitive carrier captures geochemical free energy into a transferable store with a closing ledger (proto-energetics arm, supported); a compartment internalizes electrochemical work under selection and pays for it within a Sojo-bounded survival region (internalization arm, supported); which coupling chemistry pays is structure-sensitive and membrane-leak-dependent (5-class alt arm, supported, classes agree ~46%); and a specified thioester carrier does useful work through a gradient dip where gradient-only starves but does NOT improve total useful work (sharpened Gate-E arm, og-35, qualified). The consolidated headline is bounded by that sharpest arm: qualified, dip-persistence not throughput. Every matched null and every arm verdict is preserved.

Background

Question Can a primitive chemical system capture geochemical free energy, internalize it under selection, and couple a specified carrier to perform endergonic work — and does any of it improve total throughput, or only persistence/robustness — each tested against a matched null with a closing ledger?
Hypothesis Energetic mechanisms (capture, internalization, carrier coupling) raise persistence/robustness — doing useful work through gradient dips and surviving gradient loss — but do not net more total useful work, because stores time-shift and leak and activation is gated at the carrier bond. No net benefit is claimed where an arm's ledger does not close without a hidden inflow.

Model & approach

Consolidate the proto-energetics studies into one concept with explicit fidelity-labelled arms, each preserving its matched null, verdict, findings and run provenance; report the consolidated verdict bounded by the sharpest arm (og-35, qualified) without over-claiming past it.
