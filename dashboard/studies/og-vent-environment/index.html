The Vent Environment: A Bounded but Viable Geochemical Reactor

Q Is a serpentinizing Lost City-type vent a viable geochemical reactor that supplies the H2, inorganic carbon, pH/redox gradients and electrochemical driving force downstream chemistry needs — and within what bounds, as the mineral interface evolves and across the plausible range of vent states?

Claim

Across fidelities the vent environment is a viable but bounded geochemical reactor. The serpentinizing reactor settles inside the Lost City window (50.6 C, pH 9.37, sustained H2) over ~80% of the operating grid — habitability is a bounded region, not one tuned run (og-00, supported). Conditioning on the Lost City field windows, 12.5% (6/48) of the empirically motivated prior reproduces all three observations and 100% of that posterior supplies the H2 + pH gradient downstream carbon fixation needs; dissolved H2 is the binding observation, pH is buffered and non-discriminating (og-cal, supported, calibrated). The sharpened coupled interface delivers H2 / inorganic-carbon flux + a maintained pH/redox gradient at or above required levels and a CO2-reducing electrochemical driving force, with mass/charge/energy closing across PHREEQC+FiPy+Reaktoro — but precipitation and passivation throttle supply on the mineral-evolution timescale and the supply<->throttle is a sharp two-regime DIC cliff, so delivery is bounded (og-27, canonical, qualified). Consolidated headline: qualified — a viable vent reactor within a bounded window, supplying downstream chemistry conditionally. Every matched null and arm verdict kept.

Background

Question Is a serpentinizing Lost City-type vent a viable geochemical reactor that supplies the H2, inorganic carbon, pH/redox gradients and electrochemical driving force downstream chemistry needs — and within what bounds, as the mineral interface evolves and across the plausible range of vent states?
Hypothesis A serpentinizing vent supplies the reducing power and gradients downstream chemistry needs, but within a bounded window — only a fraction of plausible vent states bind (H2-limited) and the evolving mineral interface throttles supply over time; no unconditional supply is claimed outside that window.

Model & approach

Consolidate the vent-environment studies into one concept with explicit fidelity-labelled arms (reactor baseline, calibration, coupled evolving interface), each preserving its discriminating control, verdict, findings and run provenance; report the consolidated verdict bounded by the sharpest arm (og-27, qualified) without over-claiming unconditional supply.
