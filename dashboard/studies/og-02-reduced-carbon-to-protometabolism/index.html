og-02The First Metabolism

Q Under continuous feed and washout, does a curated prebiotic reaction network persist and self-amplify as a connected system, rather than merely producing isolated organic molecules?

Overview

Claim

Under continuous feed and washout, a balanced, experimentally-constrained minimal reaction network either sustains net carbon throughput and genuine autocatalytic amplification through stoichiometrically valid reactions that survive washout, or it does not — in which case the earlier lumped-network amplification (gain peaking near 29.5) is shown to be an artifact of an element- unbalanced reaction rather than endogenous emergence. Curated persistence is no longer claimed as emergence; the corrected-curated, measured-nonenzymatic, and rule-generated arms are compared on equal footing, each held to mass/charge balance.

Background

Question Under continuous feed and washout, does a curated prebiotic reaction network persist and self-amplify as a connected system, rather than merely producing isolated organic molecules?
Hypothesis A curated graph (formate/acetate/pyruvate/glyoxylate + rTCA fragments) seeded with Muchowska/Moran edges sustains nonzero throughput and at least one autocatalytic motif within a feasible resource budget.

Model & approach

Insert an experimentally-motivated reaction graph and test whether metabolism-like subnetworks emerge under flow.

Model Reduced carbon to protometabolism (balanced rebuild)model →
  • Module E — REAL libRoadRunner/Tellurium: a curated SBML rTCA-fragment network (pyruvate carboxylation + a lumped autocatalytic reductive-TCA turn OAA+2CO2 -> 2OAA+acetate + reductive branch to malate/succinate) run as a continuous-feed / washout CSTR, advanced each tick by the genuine roadrunner ODE integrator.
  • Emergent observables (not assumptions): net carbon throughput under feed+washout; the autocatalytic gain k_rtca.[CO2]/k_wash (>1 => a reinforcing motif is active; gain peaks ~29.5 then CO2-limits); networkx connectivity of the species-reaction graph (a single connected network). A no-CO2 control collapses flux + gain to 0.
