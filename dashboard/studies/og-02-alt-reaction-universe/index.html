og-02The Open Network: Do Autocatalytic Motifs Emerge From Rule-Generated Chemistry, Beyond Chance?

Q If the prebiotic reaction network is generated from reaction rules rather than hand-curated, with real eQuilibrator thermodynamics and fed the calibrated vent state, do persistent autocatalytic motifs appear at all, and are they more frequent than in matched null networks?

Claim

Fed the calibrated vent posterior, the rule-generated universe (121 species, 413 reactions) sustains a persistent 14-species network but contains no autocatalytic motif, because every C-C-bond-forming route is uphill at vent concentrations. Supplying 10 mM formaldehyde makes exactly one motif emerge - the formose cycle, recovered post hoc and absent in all 24 matched null networks (enrichment p = 0.04). Graded by the same detector, og-02's curated rTCA motif turns out to be imposed, and its lumped turn is not element-balanced, creating 4 carbon atoms per cycle, while the generated universe's ledger closes exactly. The study demonstrates that autocatalysis is not free under real thermodynamics, but the one motif that ignites needs a formaldehyde feed the vent chemistry does not itself produce.

Background

Question If the prebiotic reaction network is generated from reaction rules rather than hand-curated, with real eQuilibrator thermodynamics and fed the calibrated vent state, do persistent autocatalytic motifs appear at all, and are they more frequent than in matched null networks?
Hypothesis Under the vent posterior feed (CO2 1.4 mM, H2 10.8 mM, pyruvate 0.1 mM) the generated universe sustains a connected multi-species network but no autocatalytic motif (the C1 -> C2 bottleneck is thermodynamic at vent concentrations); supplying the formose feedstock (formaldehyde) lets a motif emerge from the rules and that motif count is enriched relative to degree-preserving, carbon/oxygen-matched nulls; the curated class's motif is detected but imposed and its stoichiometry fails the element ledger.

Model & approach

Build the proto_metabolism face (curated_rtca | reaction_universe | null_network), the vg-data package reaction_universe (rules, eQuilibrator dG', catalyst requirements), the NetworkLedgerProcess (exact element residual, integrated carbon balance, dissipation >= 0) and a class-agnostic post-hoc motif detector; run the three classes under two feeds (vent posterior; + 10 mM formaldehyde) with 24 matched nulls per arm, an ignition map, flux-control analysis and a stiffness check.

Key assumptions

  • Linear C/H/O skeletons up to C4; eleven reaction rules (H2 reductions, enol / reductive carboxylation, formose initiation, aldol, isomerisation, alpha/beta decarboxylation, Cannizzaro); every reaction reversible with k_down = k0 (rule class) and k_up = k0 exp(-|dG'0|/RT).
  • dG' potentials at pH 9.38 / I 0.25 M / 322 K (the vent posterior example point); water activity 1; CO2 and H2 vent-buffered, organics as CSTR inflow.
  • Catalyst classes (mineral: reductions / carboxylations; base: aldol / isomerisation / Cannizzaro) share a finite site pool f = sites / (1 + load / K_sat), updated per tick.
  • Null = degree-preserving swaps of organic species within (C, O)-count classes; dG' recomputed from the same potentials; same exchange / degradation reactions.
  • A persistent autocatalytic motif = a simple cycle of flux-carrying reactions (|v| > 1e-12 M/s, species > 1e-9 M) with stoichiometric gain > 1 (Blokhuis 2020 type-I core); currency species CO2 / H2 / H2O excluded as cycle members.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • {'Linear carbon skeletons (no branched acids, no alkenes), C <= 4, C/H/O only': 'the rTCA proper cannot close in this representation.'}
  • Rate constants are tick-scale hypothetical/extrapolated with loguniform priors that were not propagated (nominal + one 6x6 map only).
  • 24 nulls per arm (p floor 0.04); nulls are (C,O)-matched so they violate H balance by construction (mean residual 9.6 atoms).
  • {'The nulls match compound count, reaction count (400 vs 413), arity, catalyst density and C/O balance but NOT the energetic span': "their dG' are recomputed from the same compound potentials to keep them thermodynamically consistent, which widens the span to 714 kJ/mol (universe 166).   • Catalyst crowding updated once per tick (operator splitting); dG' potentials fixed at the vent example condition (pH 9.38, I 0.25 M, 322 K).
