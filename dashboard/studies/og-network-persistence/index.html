Network Persistence: Does the Carbon-Fixing Network Sustain Itself?

Q Under continuous vent-realistic feed and washout, does the prebiotic carbon-fixing network persist and sustain net carbon throughput as a connected system — and does it do so by endogenous autocatalytic amplification or merely by sustained driven flux — each arm tested against a matched null with a closing ledger?

Claim

Across fidelities the prebiotic carbon-fixing network persists — it sustains net carbon throughput as a connected system under vent-realistic feed + washout (og-02, canonical, supported) — but its persistence is driven, not autocatalytic self-amplification. Graded by a common detector, og-02's curated rTCA motif is imposed and element-unbalanced (the apparent gain an artifact); the rule-generated open universe (121 species, 413 balanced reactions) sustains a 14-species persistent network with NO autocatalytic motif at the vent feed, because every C-C-forming route is uphill, and the single motif that emerges (formose) requires an exogenous formaldehyde feed the vent chemistry does not make (og-02-alt, supported, p=0.04 vs 24 matched nulls). A versioned, provenance-tracked reaction atlas with a zero-leakage write gate supplies the balanced, confidence-tiered reactions both arms draw on (og-28, supported). Consolidated headline: supported — the network persists; endogenous autocatalysis is refuted at vent feed and tracked as the og-autocatalysis line. Every matched null and arm verdict kept.

Background

Question Under continuous vent-realistic feed and washout, does the prebiotic carbon-fixing network persist and sustain net carbon throughput as a connected system — and does it do so by endogenous autocatalytic amplification or merely by sustained driven flux — each arm tested against a matched null with a closing ledger?
Hypothesis A balanced carbon-fixing network sustains net throughput and connectivity under vent feed + washout (persistence is real), but its persistence is driven rather than autocatalytic: under real thermodynamics no self-amplifying motif emerges at the vent feed without an exogenous C1 feed, so apparent gains in element-lumped curations are artifacts. No autocatalytic emergence is claimed where the ledger does not close or the motif is imposed.

Model & approach

Consolidate the network-persistence studies into one concept with explicit fidelity-labelled arms (curated minimal network, rule-generated open universe, provenance-gated atlas), each preserving its matched null, verdict, findings and run provenance; report the consolidated verdict (supported: the network persists) with the explicit guardrail that endogenous autocatalysis is not claimed here.
