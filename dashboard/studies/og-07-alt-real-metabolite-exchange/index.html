og-07Biochemical Ecology: A Community Function That No Lineage Can Perform, Carried by Real Metabolite Flux

Q If every protocell carries the same real rule-generated chemistry masked to its own catalyst repertoire, can a community of incomplete lineages realise a metabolic function no lineage performs alone, sustained by a real intermediate crossing membranes rather than a symbolic capability transfer?

Claim

A community of one carboxylating and one hydrogenating lineage holds malate - the reductive C4 branch from oxaloacetate - at 6.4 nM while neither monoculture ever makes it and a complete cell makes it at about 20x the level. The function vanishes when nothing is shared, and its oxaloacetate precursor physically crosses between the lineages, so no capability changes hands; it appears while every cell is still incomplete. It exists only above a dicarboxylate membrane permeability of ~3e-9 m/s, one to two decades above the measured passage of charged solutes through fatty-acid vesicles, so the leakiness of the boundary rather than the chemistry decides whether ecology could run on exchanged intermediates - and at ~4e-3 molecules per cell the result is a mean-field expectation that still awaits a stochastic treatment.

Background

Question If every protocell carries the same real rule-generated chemistry masked to its own catalyst repertoire, can a community of incomplete lineages realise a metabolic function no lineage performs alone, sustained by a real intermediate crossing membranes rather than a symbolic capability transfer?
Hypothesis The reductive C4 branch (oxaloacetate -> malate -> succinate) is split across the two incomplete lineages: the fixer carboxylates pyruvate to oxaloacetate but cannot reduce it, the reducer can reduce it but never makes it; in a shared pore the dicarboxylate crosses from one to the other and malate appears in the reducer lineage although neither monoculture ever holds it; cutting the sharing (each agent exchanging only with its own copy of the background) removes it; the function depends on the membrane permeability of the exchanged diacid and on the pore residence time, and it emerges while every cell is still incomplete.

Model & approach

Build the proto_ecology face (ProtocellNetworkProcess and the matched-null class — the reaction universe inside each agent, masked to a lineage repertoire), the SpatialCouplingStep onto the enrichment field with the exchange rules of a BigraphicalReactiveSystem (uptake, secretion, pore diffusion, feed / washout; every firing counted), the EcologyReducerStep that recomputes og-07's gate readouts from the agents, the EcologyElementLedgerProcess (C / N / P over agents + field) and the vg-data package cnp_resources; run the community, its two monocultures, the private (no-sharing) counterfactual, the complete-cell (generalist) arm, the segregated placement and the rewired-null arms at the canonical point, and the four arms over (diacid permeability x pore residence); grade the pre-registered gates.

Result

The community of a carboxylating and a hydrogenating lineage holds malate (c4_diacid_hydroxy) at 6.4e-9 M in its reducer agents although the fixer monoculture, the reducer monoculture and the private (no-sharing) arm hold 0 M and neither monoculture ever produces it; the generalist (complete-cell) arm holds 1.36e-7 M, so the community reaches 4.7 % of a complete cell (3.9e-3 vs 0.08 molecules per 1-fL agent: a mean-field occupancy). The best agent in the community holds 14 of the 15 product classes in the union (max completeness 0.933, mean 0.867), so the function is present while no cell is complete.

Conclusion logic — gate decision

✓ If primary tests pass:
Empirical validation: Two incomplete lineages hold malate at 6.4 nM that neither monoculture makes, the function vanishing when nothing is shared and its oxaloacetate precursor physically crossing between them, so ecology appears while every cell is still incomplete. It exists only above a dicarboxylate permeability of about 3e-9 m/s, one to two decades above the measured passage of charged solutes through fatty-acid vesicles, so boundary leakiness rather than chemistry decides whether exchange-run ecology is possible, and at about 4e-3 molecules per cell this is a mean-field expectation still awaiting a stochastic treatment.

Conclusion

One product class — malate, the reductive C4 branch — is held by the community of a carboxylating and a hydrogenating lineage and by neither alone; it vanishes without sharing, its oxaloacetate precursor crosses between the lineages, no cell is complete, and the function needs a diacid permeability of at least 3e-9 m/s; the element ledger closes on every run.

Key assumptions

  • Eight 1-fL protocells (4 fixers, 4 reducers) at fixed positions on a 4-cell pore of 10x their volume; feed CO2 1.4 mM, H2 10.8 mM, pyruvate 0.1 mM (the vent posterior point), ammonium 50 uM, phosphate 10 uM; the localization-stage surrogate enrichment profile along the pore.
  • A lineage is a subset of the twelve reaction rules; the thermal decarboxylations belong to all; fixer = CO2 reduction + carboxylations, reducer = hydrogenations; the universe (C <= 4, 413 reactions, eQuilibrator dG', detailed balance) is otherwise identical in every agent.
  • Exchange is linear permeation by size / charge class (gas 1e-5, small neutral 1e-7, large neutral 3e-8, monoacid 1e-7, diacid 1e-8, ion 1e-10 m/s at the canonical point), applied as the exact two-compartment relaxation per tick; pore diffusion 0.1/s between cells; residence 100 s.
  • Functions are product classes (chain length x carboxylation x oxidation state); held above 1 nM, produced above 1e-12 M/s gross internal production.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • Mean-field chemistry at ~4e-3 molecules per 1-fL agent (4 per 1-pL pore compartment) and ~1e-3 precursor molecules per tick between lineages; the C4 branch is sub-molecular even in a complete cell at vent feed (0.08 per fL); a Gillespie treatment (as Phase 7) is the next step and may change the result.
  • Permeabilities are size / charge classes (extrapolated from fatty-acid vesicle literature, the diacid value hypothetical); the measured charged-solute range is 1-2 decades below the emergence boundary.
  • Agents are fixed in volume and position (no growth, division or motion — the recorded decision); the pore is 4 well-mixed cells with a hypothetical inter-cell exchange rate and the enrichment field applied as a static profile.
  • The C/H/O universe (C <= 4, linear skeletons) has no N or P rules; ammonium and phosphate are taken up and conserved but not used, so the C:N:P demand is a readout, not a gate.
  • One lineage partition (carboxylation vs hydrogenation rules) and one feed; the condenser repertoire is inert at vent feed (no formaldehyde) and was not run.
  • The rewired null yields a community-only class in one of two seeds; the discrimination is in which function, not in whether a partition can split a pathway.

Discovery implications

Where this study's results leave the mechanism model — and what to investigate next.

✓ Resolved uncertainties

  • A metabolic function can be a property of a community of incomplete protocells before any cell holds the whole pathway, with the coupling being nothing but molecules crossing membranes.
  • The exchanged intermediate and the membrane permeability it needs are named (oxaloacetate; ~3e-9 m/s for a dicarboxylate), turning og-07's robustness into a testable membrane constraint.

● Remaining uncertainties

  • Whether the function survives exact low-copy-number stochastics (4e-3 molecules per femtolitre cell; 4 per picolitre compartment).
  • The real permeability of dicarboxylates through the protocell boundaries, and whether a leakier or neutral intermediate route exists.
  • What a motion model and growth / division do to the division of labour.
