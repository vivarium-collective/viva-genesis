Claim

Owning an energy store pays only where it can be charged above the load's free energy faster than it leaks, and which chemistry clears that bar at a given membrane is the real answer: a thioester/acyl-phosphate carrier internalizes from a tenth of the drive at fatty-acid-like leak, an Fe-redox store only near full activity (~0.80), a pumped H+/Na+ gradient only once the membrane is ~100x tighter to Na+ (the Sojo constraint, per individual), and a polyphosphate store not at all against a 30 kJ/mol load. The free-energy ledger closes to rounding on all 990 runs, so these are real thermodynamic thresholds rather than bookkeeping artefacts. But the answer is structure-sensitive - the classes agree on only 46% of the grid - and every per-cell number (drive turnover, maintenance, bond energies) is hypothetical and unswept beyond the nominal grid.

Background

Question With the energy-coupling mechanism placed inside a protocell as five competing model classes, at what coupling activity does owning an internal energy store pay (its benefit exceeding its leak plus maintenance), and how does that threshold depend on the carrier chemistry and the membrane leak?
Hypothesis Internalization pays only where the store can be charged above the load's free energy faster than it leaks: the thioester-class carrier (bond 35 kJ/mol, leaking by hydrolysis) pays from a low activity at the nominal membrane; the ion-gradient store drains through the Na+ leak at fatty-acid permeabilities and pays only in tight membranes; the polyphosphate store (bond 20 kJ/mol) cannot clear a 30 kJ/mol load unless nearly fully charged; the Fe-redox store sits near the load potential and is marginal. The threshold is thermodynamic (the store potential must clear the load), not a fitted rate, so the classes separate on their leak law and bond energy — a structure-sensitive answer the sweep reports as such.

Model & approach

Build the energy_coupling face (passive_gradient | h_na_gradient_leaky_membrane | thioester_acyl_phosphate | polyphosphate | fe_redox) with the per-individual autonomy readout internal / (internal + external) power, the vg-data package energy_currencies (drives, H+/Na+ permeabilities, carrier bonds and decay, per-cell turnover and maintenance), the FreeEnergyLedgerProcess (free_energy_in >= work + dissipated with the store, exact every tick) and the protocell_energetics composite; sweep passive -> active coupling across the classes, the membrane leak and the gradient state, and find where internalization pays.

Result

At the nominal membrane and gradient the thioester / acyl-phosphate store beats the passive baseline from coupling activity 0.10: its potential bond + RT ln(q/(1-q)) crosses the 30 kJ/mol load at q = 0.12 (29.6 kJ/mol) and work begins to flow; net power rises from the passive 1.95e-17 W to a maximum 2.17e-17 W at activity 0.8 (+2.1e-18 W, autonomy 0.24) and falls again at 1.0 as the machinery cost outgrows the pool-limited store. The class pays on 54 % of the whole grid and up to P_H 3.2e-6 m/s.

Key assumptions

  • One individual (radius 0.5 um) between the external ion gradient (tapped through its H+ leak, F P_H A dc pmf, captured at 0.3) and a chemical drive delivered by substrate turnover (1e-21 mol/s at full activity) that needs no leak.
  • Store potentials: chemical carriers mu(q) = bond + RT ln(q/(1-q)) (ideal mixing, relative to the resting charge); ion gradient mu(q) = n F pmf_max q (capacitor, n = 3 ions per transfer, pmf_max 0.2 V).
  • Charging needs the drive to clear mu(q) (capped exactly); work needs mu(q) to clear the 30 kJ/mol load; leaks relax toward the resting charge at hydrolysis / oxidation rates plus carrier permeation (ratio x P_H x A/V).
  • Maintenance = 1e-19 W base + 5e-18 W x activity; the passive baseline pays only the base.
  • pays = settled net power above the passive baseline at the same membrane and gradient by > 1e-21 W.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • Every per-cell number (drive turnover, maintenance, pool, draw rate, load) is hypothetical; the package's priors were not propagated (nominal grid only).
  • The store is an ideal-mixing (chemical) or capacitor (ionic) potential with first-order relaxation toward equilibrium; no explicit carrier chemistry or ion electrodiffusion.
  • The passive tap delivers work at the gradient's potential ungated by the load; the internal store's work is gated by the load (a stated asymmetry that favours the passive baseline).
  • The Fe(II)-disproportionation drive is a placeholder window (reference pending expert acceptance); the Na+/H+ permeability ratio's magnitude is swept, only its order is inferred.
  • One individual at a time; the og-05 population loop reads the same per-individual autonomy but is not re-run here.
