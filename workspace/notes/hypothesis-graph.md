# Hypothesis graph — state-space, links, provenance crosswalk

From the Campaign dossier §1 and Appendix A. The campaign represents origins as
a succession of nonequilibrium transitions; the hypothesis graph is the object
to encode.

## State-space view (stages 0–7)

| Stage | Dominant organization | Key emergent quantity | Simulation question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Ultramafic crust + water | H2, alkalinity, heat | Does serpentinization provide sustained reducing power at plausible rates? |
| 1 | Vent–ocean flow reactor | pH/redox/thermal gradients | Are useful gradients maintained at relevant in-situ T, P, salinity and geometry? |
| 2 | Mineral microcompartments | catalytic surfaces + concentration | Do pores retain and concentrate products faster than dilution/destruction removes them? |
| 3 | Protometabolic network | persistent carbon flux | Do reaction subnetworks become self-reinforcing under continuous feed? |
| 4 | Hybrid mineral-organic compartments | selective permeability | Can amphiphiles stabilize compartments without destroying access to environmental gradients? |
| 5 | Protocells | internalized free-energy conversion | Can selection favor systems progressively less dependent on vent geometry? |
| 6 | Heritable chemical systems | memory + variation | Can polymers or catalytic ensembles preserve and propagate functional organization? |
| 7 | Pre-LUCA ecology | autonomous metabolism + heredity | What innovations permit escape from the vent and diversification? |

## Mechanistic link table (A–I)

| Link | Mechanistic proposition | Evidence class | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Serpentinization generates H2, alkalinity, heat and reduced mineral phases. | Field + geochemical theory | **Strong** |
| B | Flow/mixing builds persistent pH, redox and thermal gradients across pores or precipitates. | Field analog + reactor studies + theory | Moderate; **challenged in 2026** |
| C | Fe/Ni/S/oxide minerals catalyze H2-driven CO2 reduction to biologically relevant C1–C3 compounds. | Laboratory experiments | Strong for selected conditions |
| D | Continuous flow plus surfaces and thermophoresis overcome dilution. | Transport models + laboratory analogs | Moderate–strong |
| E | Nonenzymatic chemistry organizes into metabolic-like reaction networks. | Laboratory reaction networks | Moderate; environmental matching incomplete |
| F | Amphiphile mixtures form stable, selectively permeable compartments under vent-like conditions. | Laboratory experiments | Moderate |
| G | Environmental proton gradients can be progressively internalized as membrane bioenergetics. | Comparative biochemistry + models | Plausible but indirect |
| H | Nucleotide/polymer chemistry can arise here and become heritable. | Partial analog experiments | **Weakest link** |
| I | The trajectory converges toward pre-LUCA/LUCA physiology. | Phylogenomic reconstruction | Useful boundary condition, not direct origin evidence |
