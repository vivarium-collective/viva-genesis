How to read: A protocell's chemistry changes its physical fate: product accumulation → osmotic pressure → solvent influx → volume growth → division. Sustained net growth leading to DIVISION is a BOUNDED viable band (25% of the grid, dashed boundary) — too permeable starves (the product leaks before the osmotic pressure can build), too impermeable lyses (the pressure bursts the membrane). Physical selection without an abstract fitness score.
