Does the prebiotic mixture co-localize?
Phase 4Decisionmechanistic prediction
How to read: A low co-localization index where single species still concentrate means the mixture fractionates: different solutes concentrate to different degrees set by their Soret coefficient (low-S_T solutes such as ammonium lag the strong thermophoretic precursors). Adsorption and degradation are kinetically minor over a single pore transit, so they are not the primary driver. White diamonds are resolved-FEM audit cells. Strong + uniform = a co-localized trap; strong + non-uniform = fractionating; weak = no concentration.
