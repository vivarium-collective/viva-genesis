How to read: The real FiPy thermal trap (thermophoresis + convection, Baaske 2007) concentrates the dilute feed exponentially up a confined pore (left); as the pore closes, enrichment crosses the localization onset and an open pore just washes the feed through. Localization is real and bounded — the amber band marks where the reduced exp(Pe) column climbs past what a resolved FEM confirms, so the extreme folds are a reduced-model extrapolation.
