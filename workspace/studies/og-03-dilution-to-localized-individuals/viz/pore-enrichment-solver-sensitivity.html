Enrichment is bounded: reduced column vs resolved FEM
Campaign IIIDecisionmechanistic prediction
single-tracer enrichment — reduced column vs. resolved FEM (solver-structural uncertainty)
How to read: Localization is real and bounded: where the pore is resolvable (low Péclet) FiPy's reduced exp(Pe) column and the resolved dolfinx Soret field agree to within a decade (3 of 5 swept pores). Past the resolvable Péclet they diverge — FiPy keeps climbing toward the classical >10⁶–10⁸-fold trap, while the resolved field saturates near 253× because a P1 mesh cannot resolve the sub-micron accumulation layer. The default Baaske-favorable pore sits inside that solver-sensitive region, so the extreme fold is a reduced-model extrapolation, not a resolved result. Flow-through enrichment weakens further with Péclet; the classical headline belongs to a closed / recirculating cell.
