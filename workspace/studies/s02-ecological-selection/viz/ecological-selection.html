Ecological selection — not adhesion — makes groups fitter
Mechanismfirst fidelity
Unicell fitness is identical in both arms. Adhesion alone leaves group fitness level with it (advantage ≈ 0); only size-dependent predation/settling lifts group fitness above unicell (advantage ≈ +0.15). The group-level advantage is conferred by ecological selection, not by adhesion itself (supports H1 for the advantage).
