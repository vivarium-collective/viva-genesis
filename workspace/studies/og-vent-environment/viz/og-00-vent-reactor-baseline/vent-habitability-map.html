How to read: A bounded olivine-reaction-rate × flow regime simultaneously satisfies prespecified reactor habitability criteria; outside this region one or more constraints fail. Measured quantities are the three settled outlet model OUTPUTS — temperature, pH and serpentinization H₂ source flux — each tested against a Lost City field window (40–91 °C; pH 9.0–10.8; H₂ flux > 0). The teal dashed contour is the combined PASS boundary (all three met); grey ✕ are masked failed/out-of-range simulations, grey dots the actual swept samples, ★ the baseline operating point. Domain: olivine rate 0.005–0.4 mol/step × flow 2×10⁻⁴–3×10⁻² m/s. Limit: a geochemical-endmember reactor habitability map, not a flow-resolved field or an origin-of-life claim; per-parameter uncertainty is reported separately in the calibration arm.
