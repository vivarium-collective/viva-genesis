Contact confers the exchange advantage — realised exchange collapses with distance
Mechanismmechanistic prediction
In the screened-diffusion regime a contact partner (d=1) receives ≈49× the realised metabolite exchange of a distal one (d=10); the advantage falls off steeply with distance (log axis) and collapses only when the metabolite is well-mixed. Proximity is mechanistically what buys the exchange.
