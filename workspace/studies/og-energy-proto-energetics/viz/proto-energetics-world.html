An energy currency before modern pumping — a real free-energy-ledger composite. An exergonic geochemical drive charges a transferable carrier (teal) that is discharged to do endergonic work under load (amber) and bled by hydrolysis (red). The store reaches a steady charge it holds against the drain — a spendable currency. With no coupling or a drive too weak to clear the carrier bond, the pool never charges (the ablations).
